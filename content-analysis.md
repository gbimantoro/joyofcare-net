## 7 Core Flaws Across All 4 Articles & How to Fix Them

### 1. Thin Content Risk (Google Helpful Content / YMYL Threshold)
- **Issue**: Medical queries are classified as **YMYL (Your Money or Your Life)**. Search engines and AI algorithms demand authoritative, thorough coverage. Articles averaging 350 words are at high risk of being flagged as thin content.
- **Fix**: Expand each article to **750–1,100 words** by adding:
  - Clinical statistics (e.g., prevalence of osteoporosis in Indonesia, fracture recurrence rates).
  - Diagnostic criteria (DEXA T-score ranges explained).
  - Patient case examples or clinical workflows.
  - Dedicated FAQ sections with 3–5 high-volume queries each.

### 2. File Slug Truncation Bug
- **Issue**: Every filename and implied URL slug cuts off prematurely:
  - `bahaya-komplikasi-osteoporosis-dan-faktor-risiko-yang-wajib-` -> incomplete
  - `jenis-terapi-osteoporosis-lengkap-dari-obat-oral-injeksi-hin` -> truncated at `hin`
  - `layanan-terapi-infus-injeksi-osteoporosis-di-rumah-pasien-ho` -> truncated at `ho`
  - `panduan-fisioterapi-di-rumah-untuk-pasien-osteoporosis-mengu` -> truncated at `mengu`
- **Fix**: Define explicit, canonical slugs before publishing:
  - `bahaya-komplikasi-osteoporosis-dan-faktor-risiko-lansia`
  - `jenis-terapi-osteoporosis-obat-oral-injeksi-infus`
  - `layanan-infus-injeksi-osteoporosis-di-rumah-homecare`
  - `panduan-fisioterapi-osteoporosis-di-rumah-mencegah-jatuh`

### 3. Raw Keyword Leakage in Published Text
- **Issue**: Line 2 of all text files contains `Target Keyword / Search Intent: ...`.
- **Fix**: Ensure the publishing pipeline extracts line 2 into the CMS metadata/SEO fields and strips it completely from rendered HTML/Markdown.

### 4. Severe Lack of Internal Linking (Isolated Island Pages)
- **Issue**: None of the 4 articles reference one another or JoyofCare's core service pages.
- **Fix**: Implement a formal **Cluster Hub-and-Spoke Linking Architecture** (detailed in the next section).

### 5. Incomplete CTAs and Absence of Direct WhatsApp Deep Links
- **Issue**: Placeholders like `[Hubungi WhatsApp Resmi Joy of Care]` lack telephone numbers, formatted clickable links, or UTM tracking.
- **Fix**: Replace all placeholders with actionable WhatsApp Click-to-Chat URLs with encoded pre-filled text.

### 6. Missing Medical Reviewer Bylines (E-E-A-T Deficiency)
- **Issue**: Google requires clear authoritativeness for health content. Articles without a stated doctor/medical reviewer or clinical references lose ranking power.
- **Fix**: Add a standardized Medical Review box:
  > *Ditinjau oleh: Tim Medis Joy of Care (dr. [Nama Dokter], Sp.PD / Sp.OT)*  
  > *Terakhir diperbarui: 5 September 2026 | Referensi: PEROSI, WHO, IOF*

### 7. Layout & Whitespace Formatting Glitches
- **Issue**: Article 2 contains over 30 lines of empty whitespace between list items.
- **Fix**: Run a markdown normalization script to remove redundant empty lines.
