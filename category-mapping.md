## Category Naming Conventions

### Rules:
1. **2-3 words maximum** in Bahasa Indonesia
2. **Use hyphens** for multi-word categories
3. **Lowercase only** (no capital letters)
4. **No numbers** in category names
5. **Match search keywords** (not arbitrary names)
6. **Avoid abbreviations** unless widely recognized

### Examples:
- ✅ `perawatan-lansia` (not `elderly-care` or `lansia`)
