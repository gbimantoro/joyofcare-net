# JoyofCare Social Media Campaign (60 Days)

**Date:** 5 September 2026  
**Strategist:** JoC Strategist  
**Duration:** 60 days (September 8 - November 6, 2026)  
**Platforms:** Instagram, Facebook, YouTube Shorts, Twitter/X

---

## Campaign Overview

### Objective
Increase brand awareness and engagement for JoyofCare through consistent social media content across all platforms, driving traffic to the website and generating WhatsApp leads.

### Target Audience
- Indonesian families caring for elderly parents (lansia)
- Patients with chronic conditions (Parkinson, osteoporosis, stroke)
- Students studying abroad and their families
- Health-conscious individuals in Jakarta, Tangerang, Depok, Bogor

### Key Metrics
- **Reach:** 100,000+ monthly impressions
- **Engagement:** 3-5% engagement rate
- **Leads:** 50+ WhatsApp inquiries per month
- **Followers:** +20% growth in 60 days

## Platform-Specific Guidelines

### Instagram
- **Feed Posts:** High-quality images, 1080x1080px or 1080x1350px
- **Carousels:** 5-10 slides, consistent design
- **Reels:** 9:16 aspect ratio, 15-60 seconds
- **Stories:** Use stickers, polls, questions
- **Bio:** Link to WhatsApp or Linktree

### Facebook
- **Posts:** 1200x630px images, engaging captions
- **Reels:** Cross-post from Instagram
- **Groups:** Join health-related groups, share valuable content
- **Page:** Complete all information, enable messaging

### YouTube
- **Shorts:** 9:16 aspect ratio, 60 seconds max
- **Thumbnails:** Bold text, bright colors
- **Descriptions:** Include links and hashtags
- **Tags:** Health, homecare, Jakarta, etc.

### Twitter/X
- **Threads:** 3-5 tweets, hook first tweet
- **Single Posts:** Short, punchy, with images
- **Hashtags:** 2-3 per tweet max
- **Engagement:** Reply to comments, retweet relevant content
